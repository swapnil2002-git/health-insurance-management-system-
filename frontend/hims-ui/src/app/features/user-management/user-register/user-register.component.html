Role & Activation Policy

CUSTOMER

Customer accounts are auto-activated immediately upon creation. Policyholders can log in right away.

STAFF / OPERATIONAL ROLES

Accounts with internal operational roles (Agents, Underwriters, Claims Officers, Administrators) are registered with PENDING status.

An administrator must explicitly approve these accounts under Authorize User before login credentials will function.
